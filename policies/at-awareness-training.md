
                    AWARENESS AND TRAINING POLICY

  Classification:          CUI / FOUO

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes the security awareness and training requirements for
TeNova Axiom to ensure that all personnel understand their security
responsibilities and are adequately trained to protect organizational
information systems and Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). This policy
implements the requirements of NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5, AT family controls
(AT-1 through AT-4), CMMC v2.0 Practice AT.L2-3.2.1, and NIST SP 800-171
requirements 3.2.1, 3.2.2, and 3.2.3.

_______________________________________________________________________________


2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, subcontractors, and
third-party personnel who access TeNova Axiom information systems, process
Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), or operate within the authorization
boundary of any system governed by NIST 800-53 or CMMC v2.0. This includes
personnel at all organizational levels regardless of whether access is
physical, logical, remote, or temporary.

_______________________________________________________________________________


3. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

  Authorizing Official (AO)
      Approves the security awareness and training policy. Reviews and
      endorses the annual training program. Ensures adequate resources are
      allocated for training delivery and recordkeeping.

  Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
      Designated policy owner. Develops and maintains the training curriculum.
      Ensures training content reflects current threats, regulatory changes,
      and organizational risk posture. Reports training compliance metrics
      to the AO quarterly.

  Information System Security Officer (ISSO)
      Enforces training completion requirements. Validates that personnel
      have completed required training prior to granting or renewing system
      access. Conducts periodic spot checks of training records.

  Human Resources (HR)
      Coordinates new-hire onboarding to include security awareness training
      enrollment. Notifies ISSO of personnel role changes requiring updated
      role-based training. Maintains employment records cross-referenced
      with training completion data.

  System Administrators
      Complete role-based training specific to system administration duties.
      Verify training completion before provisioning elevated access
      privileges. Report training gaps to the ISSO.

  All Personnel
      Complete all assigned security awareness and role-based training within
      required timeframes. Report suspected security incidents as instructed
      during training. Acknowledge understanding of security responsibilities
      through signed attestation.

4. ATTESTATION REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Security Awareness Policy (AT-1)

    TeNova Axiom shall maintain a documented security awareness and training
    policy that addresses purpose, scope, roles, responsibilities,
    management commitment, coordination among organizational entities, and
    compliance.

    The policy shall be reviewed and updated at least annually or whenever
    significant organizational or regulatory changes occur. Each review
    shall be documented with the date, reviewer, and a summary of changes
    made. The CISO shall attest to the currency of this policy annually.

4.2 General Awareness Training (AT-2)

    All personnel shall complete security awareness training before being
    granted access to organizational information systems and annually
    thereafter. Training content shall include, at a minimum:

    (a)  Phishing identification and reporting procedures
    (b)  Social engineering attack recognition
    (c)  CUI handling, marking, and transmission requirements
    (d)  Incident reporting procedures and contact information
    (e)  Acceptable use of organizational information systems
    (f)  Physical security awareness (tailgating, clean desk, visitor escort)
    (g)  Password and authentication best practices
    (h)  Removable media and mobile device security
    (i)  Insider threat indicators and reporting channels

    New hires shall complete awareness training within 5 business days of
    onboarding. Personnel who fail to complete annual refresher training
    within 30 days of the due date shall have system access suspended until
    training is completed. The CISO shall attest annually that all active
    personnel have completed the required training.

4.3 Role-Based Security Training (AT-3)

    Personnel assigned security-significant roles shall receive specialized
    training specific to their assigned duties. Role-based training is
    required for the following positions:

    (a)  Privileged users (system administrators, database administrators)
    (b)  Information System Security Officers (ISSOs)
    (c)  Security operations and incident response personnel
    (d)  Developers and configuration managers
    (e)  Auditors and assessors with system access

    Role-based training shall be completed within 30 days of role assignment
    and refreshed annually. Training content shall be tailored to the
    specific technical and procedural responsibilities of each role.
    Personnel transferring between security roles shall complete the
    role-based training for their new position before assuming duties.
    The ISSO shall attest that all personnel in security-significant roles
    have current, role-appropriate training.

4.4 Training Records (AT-4)

    TeNova Axiom shall maintain individual training records that document
    at a minimum:

    (a)  Personnel name and organizational identifier
    (b)  Training course title and version
    (c)  Date of completion
    (d)  Method of delivery (classroom, computer-based, exercise)
    (e)  Assessment score or pass/fail status where applicable
    (f)  Instructor or training provider identification

    Records shall be retained for the duration of employment plus one year
    following separation. Training records shall be accessible for audit
    and inspection by authorized assessors within 24 hours of request.
    The ISSO shall attest quarterly that training records are current,
    complete, and properly stored.

5. ATTESTATION VERIFICATION

Compliance with this policy is verified through periodic attestation review.
These controls are organizational in nature and are not subject to automated
technical scanning. The ISSO and CISO shall verify compliance through
documented review at the intervals specified below.

  Control     Description                     Review Interval
  --------    ----------------------------    ---------------
  AT-1.1      Security Awareness Policy       365 days
  AT-2.1      Security Awareness Training     365 days
  AT-3.1      Role-Based Security Training    365 days
  AT-4.1      Training Records                365 days

Each attestation review shall produce a signed attestation record documenting
the reviewer, date of review, findings, and any corrective actions required.
All attestation results are recorded as SWT3 Witness Anchors in the
Compliance Ledger with SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprints, providing
tamper-evident proof of continuous compliance.

_______________________________________________________________________________


6. ENFORCEMENT

Violations of this policy may result in:

    (a)  Immediate suspension of system access pending training completion
    (b)  Disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment
    (c)  Removal of contractor personnel from the contract
    (d)  Reporting to the contracting officer for contract-related breaches
    (e)  Referral to law enforcement for willful negligence resulting in
         compromise of CUI

_______________________________________________________________________________


7. REVIEW AND MAINTENANCE

This policy shall be reviewed annually by the Chief Information Security
Officer (CISO) and updated as necessary to reflect changes in organizational
structure, regulatory requirements, threat landscape, or training delivery
capabilities. All reviews and updates are witnessed into the Axiom Compliance
Ledger under control AT-1.1.
